Pin Faux Spy to your toolbar

Click the Extensions icon (the puzzle piece) next to the address bar, then click the pin next to Faux Spy. Then clicking the Faux Spy icon shows your scans left today and lets you scan a whole page.

2

Check a photo, three ways

  • Hover over a photo and click Investigate.
  • Right-click any image and choose Investigate this image.
  • Ctrl + click an image for an instant check.

Works on dating apps, social media and any other site you open in your browser.
